# Roblox Audio

## Purpose

Use this module for every player-facing sound decision.

## Procedure

1. Define the role of the sound.
2. Choose the minimum set of cues that support the gameplay.
3. Separate ambience, effects, music, and voice.
4. Check loudness, repetition, and clarity in playtest.
5. Confirm audio still works in the target scene and device context.

## Rules

- Audio should support gameplay, not overwhelm it.
- Important effects must be distinct.
- Avoid repetitive or fatiguing loops.
- Keep cues short and readable when they matter for mechanics.

## Review checklist

- The audio matches the game’s tone.
- Important cues are recognizable.
- Nothing is too loud or too repetitive.
- The mix supports the player experience.
