# Roblox Lighting

## Purpose

Use this module for visual mood and scene clarity.

## Procedure

1. Decide the mood and gameplay readability goal.
2. Set the baseline lighting before adding effects.
3. Add atmosphere, color, and contrast carefully.
4. Check that the scene still reads well for players.
5. Re-test after changing exposure or post-processing.

## Rules

- Do not sacrifice gameplay readability for style.
- Use lighting to guide attention.
- Keep night scenes navigable.
- Avoid overprocessing that makes the scene muddy.

## Review checklist

- Players can read the environment.
- Important objects remain visible.
- The mood matches the experience.
- The lighting does not fight the UI.
