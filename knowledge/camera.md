# Roblox Camera

## Purpose

Use this module for camera behavior and viewpoint design.

## Procedure

1. Decide the camera style needed by the game.
2. Keep the view readable and comfortable.
3. Separate camera logic from gameplay logic.
4. Test transitions, zoom, and motion comfort.
5. Confirm the camera works across the intended devices.

## Rules

- The camera must not fight the player.
- Avoid motion that causes confusion or discomfort.
- Keep important targets in view.
- Match camera behavior to the genre.

## Review checklist

- The player can understand the view.
- The camera feels stable.
- The view supports gameplay.
- The camera does not block important information.
