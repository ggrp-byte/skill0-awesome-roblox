# Roblox Building

## Purpose

Use this module for physical spaces, props, and world structure.

## Procedure

1. Identify the gameplay function of the area.
2. Block out the space before polishing details.
3. Check scale, navigation, and sightlines.
4. Add detail only after the layout works.
5. Keep the scene readable for players.

## Rules

- Build with gameplay first, decoration second.
- Keep paths clear.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity in collision-heavy areas.
- Reuse shapes and patterns when possible.
- Match art style to the game’s tone.

## Review checklist

- The space supports the intended gameplay.
- The player path is obvious.
- The area is not overloaded with unnecessary parts.
- The composition reads well from common camera angles.
